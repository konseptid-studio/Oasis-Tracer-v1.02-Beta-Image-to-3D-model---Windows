Oasis Tracer_v1.02 Beta is a lightweight, offline Windows application designed to
turn flat vector illustrations, cartoons, logos, photographs or pop-art into high-quality,
ready-to-print 3D models and cutter-ready SVG outlines in seconds.

Simple. Fast. Made for real-world 3D printing and cutting.
No subscription. No cloud. No bloat. Just drop your image, tweak a few settings if needed,
and get quality STLs, 3MF, and SVG files ready for your machines.

What it does:

Loads common image formats (PNG, JPG with alpha transparency), and other formats
Intelligently separates the image into clean color regions using advanced quantization, blending, and post-processing
Converts regions into precise vector polygons while preserving holes and fine details
Extrudes each region into solid 3D models with customizable thickness
Exports one clean STL file per color or blend, plus SVG outlines for laser cutters and CNC,
and fully assembled 3MF files
Blends 3-4 flat layers using internal layering, to give another perspective to image>3D printing
The program is optimized for creating stable STL models. Even if some are non-manifold, the models are constrained in their own
zone and do not affect the quality or the slicing. Optimized for low resource and fast generation of stable models.

Key Features:

Fast performance — processes high-resolution (up to 5000px) images and exports all files in seconds
Flexible color and layer blending system with TD — supports both sharp regions and smooth colour transitions
High working resolution for capturing fine details even from complex photos
Smart clean-up with island removal, majority smoothing, and polygon simplification
Exports SVG outlines perfect for laser cutters, CNC machines, and vinyl/foil cutters (ideal for T-shirt printing and signage)
Creates Light box add-ons, so you can light up you design.
Exports 3MF files with all parts properly grouped and ready for Bambu Studio
Runtime-editable color palettes (palette.txt and td_palette.json) — easily add or modify your preferred filament colors and names
Colors and blends are suggested based on the image, but never hard-coded — full manual control remains in your slicer

Why it works so well for 3D printing:

Every region is exported as a separate model. This gives you complete freedom in the slicer to:

Delete unwanted parts
Scale individual segments in Z (different heights)
Apply different textures, infills, or surface finishes per piece
Quickly assign filaments for multi-colour or multi-material prints (Bambu AMS, Prusa XL, etc.)

Perfect for:

Artists and makers turning photos, logos, game sprites, and pop-art into 3D prints.
Laser cutting and vinyl/foil applications for T-shirts, stickers, and signage
Anyone who wants fast print-ready results without manual clean-up.
